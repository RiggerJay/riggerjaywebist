Books I think you should get

  • Shibari You Can Use (basics to see if you like)
  • The Little Guide to Getting Tied Up: Tips for Rope Bondage Bottoms (even if you are doing the tying)
  • Essence of Shibari (deeper dive into fundamentals)

Commonly suggested sites for Learning

  • Shibari Study
  • Remedial Ropes
  • Crash Restraint
  • Rope 365
  • The Duchy

Bare Bones Suggested starting rope kit

  • 4 to 6 pieces of 25-30 foot rope in either (Hemp , Jute or Nylon)
  • 2 to 4 pieces of 15 foot rope in either (Hemp , Jute or Nylon)
  • A set of safety shears or rescue hook
